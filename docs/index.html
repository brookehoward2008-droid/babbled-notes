Lilt

Hum into readable music code.

Try a demo

 

Audio digest

The local build can turn a short WAV into the timing and pitch facts Gemma receives before it writes the Lilt program. 

python -m lilt.cli digest clip.wav
python -m lilt.cli audio clip.wav --digest clip.digest.json --backend gemini

What is this?

Hum, beatbox, whistle, or play a phrase. Lilt turns it into a short, human-readable program. Edit the program. Hear the result. Share the text.

Built for the Gemma 4 challenge by Brooke Chauntel. The model and the audio pipeline live offline; this page is the in-browser preview.

Lilt is open source under Apache-2.0. No tracking, no analytics, no account. The audio plays locally in your browser.
